# Legal texts

Put the lawyer-reviewed texts here as `termos.md`, `privacidade.md` and
`cookies.md`. They are only rendered when `LEGAL_APPROVED=true`. Until then the
pages show a "Rascunho — conteúdo jurídico por rever" outline in preview builds
and a short "em preparação" notice in public builds. Never place unreviewed
text here.
