# Legal texts

Put the lawyer-reviewed texts here: pt-AO as `termos.md`, `privacidade.md` and
`cookies.md`, English as `en/termos.md`, `en/privacidade.md` and
`en/cookies.md`. They are only rendered when `LEGAL_APPROVED=true`. Until then
the pages show a "Rascunho — conteúdo jurídico por rever" outline in preview
builds and a short notice in public builds. Never place unreviewed text here.
Confirm with the lawyer which language version prevails.
